## 🖌 Overview

Luminar Neo AI PhotoLab is an advanced photo editing application that merges artificial intelligence with creative design freedom. Developed by Skylum, it’s built for photographers, content creators, and retouchers who need intelligent automation without losing manual precision. The software streamlines editing with tools that understand light, texture, and perspective.  

The platform introduces a fully adaptive visual workflow where each layer and adjustment remains non-destructive. Real-time rendering and GPU acceleration make editing large RAW files seamless. Luminar Neo’s interface is designed for intuitive use — allowing users to focus on artistic composition instead of technical complexity.  

Powered by AI-based core modules such as Relight AI, Sky Replacement AI, and Portrait Bokeh AI, the program automatically detects subjects, adjusts depth, and balances exposure with professional accuracy. It can rebuild lighting in flat images, restore overexposed skies, and enhance portraits with realistic focus depth simulation.  

Luminar Neo’s modular architecture integrates creative extensions, enabling advanced functions like background removal, noise reduction, and texture enhancement. The AI-powered masking tools precisely isolate objects and surfaces, offering full editing control without complex selections.  

Used by professionals and hobbyists alike, Luminar Neo excels in visual storytelling, from fine art photography to commercial post-production. It bridges speed and creativity — transforming digital editing into an expressive process where technology enhances, not replaces, the artist’s vision.
